## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-01 | Tampering | `POST /api/external/lessons/:id/:action` body | high | mitigate | D-06 `safeParse` against the registry shape for every tool-backed action, before method logic; `set_plan` with a non-array `nodes`, `node_status` with an unknown status and `remember` with a missing fact are all rejected 400 instead of reaching the database |
