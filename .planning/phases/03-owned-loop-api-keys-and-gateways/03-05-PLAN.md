<objective>
**As a** learner on a free-tier or rate-limited key, **I want to** see that the provider is busy and have Derive wait for it, **so that** a rate limit costs me a minute rather than a lesson — and **as a** learner whose lesson has grown very long, **I want to** be told so plainly with a next step, **so that** I am never taught without the understanding gate.

This refines the loop's slice with its three failure directions: the provider says wait, the loop runs away, and the prompt outgrows the window. All three end through the one `turn_end` the browser already knows, and none of them adds an event type.

Purpose: make the two ways a turn can stop being useful — a stall and an overlong lesson — both end in Derive's own words with a next step.
Output: the retry controller with its visible countdown, the pre-request headroom refusal, the step-cap continuable end, the one-link error item, and the suites that cover each.
</objective>

  <action>
Own the backoff in the loop, show it as an ephemeral status line, and let the provider's own words carry the failure when the window runs out.

**1. The constants.** Export `RETRY_WINDOW_MS = 120_000` and `RETRY_BASE_MS = 1_000`, each with a one-sentence `//` comment in the voice `schedule.ts` uses beside its floor: two minutes because a free-tier Gemini or OpenRouter rate limit can be a full minute and a lesson that dies on it is a dead lesson, and a learner who does not want to wait presses Stop. Also export `STEP_CAP = 400`, moving plan 03-01's inline literal onto a named constant so the suite can read it.

**2. `retryAfterMs(e)`.** Narrow with `APICallError.isInstance(e)` and read `e.responseHeaders?.['retry-after-ms']` first, then `e.responseHeaders?.['retry-after']`, accepting either a seconds count or an HTTP date for the latter. Return `null` when neither is present or neither parses. Honour the value as given rather than capping it at 60 s — the SDK's own retry caps it, which is precisely why the SDK's retry is off.

**3. `retryDelay(attempt, afterMs)`.** When `afterMs` is a number, return it. Otherwise exponential backoff with jitter: `RETRY_BASE_MS * 2 ** attempt` plus a random fraction of that same interval, so two lessons hitting the same limit do not retry in lockstep. Clamp the returned delay so the cumulative wait never exceeds `RETRY_WINDOW_MS`.

**4. `withRetries(run, sink, ctx)`.** Wrap the whole `streamText` attempt, not an individual step: on a thrown or `error`-part failure, decide whether to retry by `APICallError.isInstance(e) && e.isRetryable === true`, or by `e.statusCode` being 429 or in the 5xx range when `isRetryable` is absent. A non-retryable failure propagates immediately. While waiting, emit the countdown once per second through `sink.emitEphemeral('status', { text: \`Provider busy, retrying in ${n}s\` })` with `n = Math.ceil(remaining / 1000)`, and switch to `sink.emitEphemeral('status', { text: 'Provider busy, retrying now' })` for the moment the next attempt is in flight. No attempt counter: `attempt 3 of 6` is machinery the learner cannot act on. Check `ctx.isStopping()` on every tick and before each attempt and return immediately when it is true, so Stop is honoured without waiting out the countdown; also race the sleep against the abort signal so a Stop during a wait is instant rather than up to a second late.

**5. Exhaustion.** When the cumulative wait reaches `RETRY_WINDOW_MS`, stop retrying and end the turn through the existing error path: build the sentence with `providerSentence(e.statusCode ?? 0, e.responseBody ?? e.message)` and the hint with `hintFor(labelOf(provider), e.statusCode ?? 0, sentence)`, then `sink.endTurn({ ok: false, error: hint ? \`${sentence}\n${hint}\` : sentence })`. The provider's words reach the learner unchanged, with Derive's fix hint on its own line beside them, which is what `probe.ts` already does on the settings page. Pass everything through `safeMessage` on the way out, the way plan 03-01's `catch` already does.

**6. Keep the ephemeral line out of the record.** The countdown is `emitEphemeral` only — never `emit`, never `checkpoint` — so it is fanned out at `seq: -1` and never written to the event log, replayed by the SSE stream or mirrored into the Obsidian note. A transient stall is not part of what the learner learned. Add no `EVENT_TYPES` entry and no `applyEvent` branch; `web/src/lib/useLesson.ts` is not edited by this plan.

**7. Keep `Thinking` and the tool labels as they are.** The `reasoning-start` handler and the tool-label status line from plan 03-01 are untouched: `Thinking`, the `TOOL_LABELS` entry for whichever tool is in flight, and the retry countdown are three uses of one existing event, which is the whole reason D-09, D-10 and D-17 fit under the event-vocabulary ceiling.
