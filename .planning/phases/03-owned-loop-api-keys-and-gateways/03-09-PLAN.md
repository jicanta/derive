<objective>
**As a** learner on a provider with no search of its own, **I want to** give Derive a way to check a fact from one small block in Settings, **so that** the tutor keeps the verification discipline the method depends on — and **as a** learner who disagrees with Derive's prices, **I want to** be told where to put my own, **so that** the figure on my screen is one I can correct.

This closes the phase: the one new Settings card and the one new Settings sentence, the two config surfaces that document them, and the HTTP suite running through the owned loop with a stub model, which is the automated half of Success Criterion 1.

Purpose: make the two things a learner configures for this phase reachable in one place, and prove the whole owned loop through the API suite with no key.
Output: the lifted field module, the web-search card, the prices sentence, the `.env.example` and doctor lines, and `server/test/api.test.ts` running on the owned loop.
</objective>
