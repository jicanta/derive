<objective>
Voice stops making the learner wait for a paragraph, and starts speaking in the learner's language. Sentences are spoken as they complete while the block is still arriving, the mic keeps arming only after the block's last sentence, and `prefs.language` is resolved to a BCP-47 tag through a committed table rather than guessed from the browser's installed voices.

Purpose: the latency win is the only reason to touch voice at all (D-18), and a tutor that speaks English to a learner who asked for Spanish is not teaching that learner. Both halves are pure functions plus one hook, so both are testable with no browser — which is what makes `voice.test.ts` (D-27's third gate) worth having.

Output: `web/src/lib/locales.ts` as committed pure data, `setVoiceLang` and an exported sentence splitter in `web/src/lib/voice.ts`, streaming sentence chunks with a mic guard in `web/src/lib/useVoiceMode.ts`, and `server/test/voice.test.ts`.
</objective>

<decisions_resolved_here>
Recorded as the planner's call in CONTEXT.md §Claude's Discretion.

- **The sentence-splitting rule (D-18).** Split after `.`, `!`, `?`, `…`, `。`, `！` or `？` when the character is followed by whitespace or end-of-string, **unless** the character is a `.` immediately preceded by a digit (a decimal, `3.14`), or preceded by a single letter plus `.` repeated (an initialism, `e.g.`, `U.S.`), or preceded by one of a short committed abbreviation list (`e.g`, `i.e`, `etc`, `vs`, `cf`, `Dr`, `Mr`, `Mrs`, `Ms`, `Fig`, `No`, `approx`). A completed sentence shorter than **24 characters** is not spoken on its own: it waits and is prepended to the next one, so `Yes.` does not become its own utterance. The splitter returns only *complete* sentences and the index of the first unconsumed character, so the caller never speaks a trailing fragment.
- **The locale table's initial entries (D-19).** English names and endonyms for the languages Derive's own copy and the method already reach for: English, Spanish/español, Portuguese/português (plus Brazilian Portuguese → `pt-BR`), French/français, German/Deutsch, Italian/italiano, Dutch/Nederlands, Swedish/svenska, Polish/polski, Turkish/Türkçe, Greek/ελληνικά, Catalan/català, Russian/русский, Ukrainian/українська, Hebrew/עברית, Arabic/العربية, Hindi/हिन्दी, Japanese/日本語, Korean/한국어, Mandarin and Chinese/中文 (`zh-CN`). Matching is case-folded and accent-tolerant on the lookup key, trimmed, and bounded by the 60-character `prefs.language` limit. A name absent from the table resolves to `null` and the caller falls back to `navigator.language` — absent is unknown, never a guess, which is the discipline `server/src/pricing.ts` states for prices.
- **Where the hook gets the tag.** `useVoiceMode` takes the resolved tag as an option. The one `api.stats()` call that Lesson.tsx gains for the due pill is where `prefs.language` comes from, and that wiring lands in `05-07` — RESEARCH.md §2.8 is explicit that this must be one fetch serving two decisions, not two fetches. This plan ships the seam and its test; `05-07` connects it.
</decisions_resolved_here>
