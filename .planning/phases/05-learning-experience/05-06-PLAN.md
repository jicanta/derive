<objective>
Remediation stops being something the model may or may not do well. The server ranks the failed node's own dependencies, names exactly one, refuses a quiz anywhere else until that one is re-checked or the learner speaks, and hands the tutor the learner's own recorded misconceptions as distractor material at the moment the question is being written. The review and warm-up turn prompts are tightened so the next due question follows the previous verdict without prose in between.

Purpose: `method/40-discipline.md`'s "targeted remediation, not re-explanation" and `method/20-tools.md`'s node-status rules are sentences this makes literally true. Deterministic, testable with no model, and it holds on a weak local model — the one most likely to wander.

Output: `weakestFirst` in `server/src/schedule.ts`; the one-node instruction, the refusal and its two exits in `server/src/actions.ts`; a per-node misconception read in `server/src/db.ts`; strengthened review and warm-up turn prompts in `server/src/prompt.ts`; a fourth conformance verdict; and no-model HTTP gates for all of it.
</objective>

<decisions_resolved_here>
- **D-20 extends to an ordinary failed quiz (RESEARCH Open Question 3, operator decision: extend it).** The dependency-naming helper has exactly two call sites today — the cumulative-miss instruction and the shaky branch of node-status — and the plain-check miss branches name no dependency at all. But Success Criterion 4 and LEARN-09 both read "on a failed quiz", and that is the question verification will ask. **Three instruction strings instead of two.**
- **Where the ranking lives (D-21 discretion).** `weakestFirst` goes in `server/src/schedule.ts` as a pure function over row-shaped inputs, because that module has no I/O and imports only a type from the database layer — which is why its test opens no file. The two tie-breaks need data the scheduler cannot see, so they ride in as precomputed fields on each input row: a boolean for whether this lesson's checks on that dependency showed a miss, and the dependency's status. The caller in `server/src/actions.ts` gathers both from reads the dependency-naming helper already performs, so this adds an ordering, not a query.
- **How the gate's open state is held (D-20/D-22 discretion).** A module-level map in `server/src/actions.ts`, keyed by lesson id, holding the named node id — in the shape of the shipped deferred-message registry, and documented as module-level state that is the module's purpose. **It does not survive a restart, and that is correct:** the gate is a within-turn instruction to a model whose turn did not survive either, and the instruction is re-derived from the same ranking on the next miss. This spends no migration, which matters because `05-01` already spends one.
- **Resolved misconceptions are distractor material, and the read is lesson-scoped (PATTERNS open decision 2).** Marking a node locked flips its misconceptions to resolved, so by the time a node is being taught again its prior misconceptions all carry that flag — and a resolved misconception is the *best* distractor, because it is a known-tempting wrong claim the learner has actually held. The read therefore drops the shipped resolved-first ordering. It is scoped to the lesson, not followed across lessons through the source-node link: a misconception's node id is unique only within its lesson, so a cross-lesson match would be a guess.
- **D-17 is in-turn pipelining (operator decision).** "The next card is prefetched" is read as the tutor asking the next question inside the same turn with no prose in between, not as preparing a card before showing it. A turn throws when another is active, one turn per lesson is a named architectural constraint, and every card-producing tool emits its card immediately — so there is no prepare-without-showing path to build against. This lands as a turn-prompt change only: **no new tool, no flag on the quiz tool, no held-but-unshown card, no method-surface change and no new conformance assertion.** It narrows LEARN-11 one level further than D-17 already narrowed it, and that narrowing is recorded here rather than silently matched. The two turn prompts being tightened are hand-written in `server/src/prompt.ts` and are not render targets, so the method-drift gate is not involved.
- **D-24 is verified, not rebuilt.** The warm-up eyebrow and the quiz card's cumulative, review and pre-explanation headings with their three what-it-tests labels are shipped. This plan asserts them against LEARN-09 and spends its effort on the three decisions above.
</decisions_resolved_here>
